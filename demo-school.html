Leadership Message

Principal's Welcome Note

Dear Parents and Students,

It gives me immense pleasure to welcome you to Eastern Public School, Jajmau, Kanpur. Every child enters school with boundless curiosity and unique potential. Our responsibility as educators is to nurture that spark with patience, discipline, and joyful learning experiences.

Beyond textbooks and examinations, we place strong emphasis on spoken English fluency, digital literacy, hands-on science exhibitions, cultural celebrations, and moral upbringing. Together with parents, we strive to shape confident young individuals who make their families and Kanpur proud.

We warmly invite you to visit our Jajmau campus and experience our vibrant learning environment firsthand.
